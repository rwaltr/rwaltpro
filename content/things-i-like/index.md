## Mindset

The principles I actually try to work and live by, with the receipts.

### How I build

**Simplicity, not "simple."** Simplicity is expensive. It's what's left after you pick one way to do a thing and delete the rest. Every app in [home-ops](/homelab-stack/) has the same shape for exactly this reason: uniformity beats cleverness, and clever is what pages you at 3am.

**If it isn't in git, it doesn't exist.** The cluster reconciles from a repo, the dotfiles from chezmoi, the host from Ignition. Nothing important lives only inside a running machine, because running machines are disposable.

**Test before you trust.** Infrastructure changes get exercised in a throwaway VM before they touch the real host, and Terraform gets a `plan` before an `apply`. I learned that one the expensive way.

**The basics, elevated.** 3D printing taught me the lesson I keep coming back to: the basics, done well, beat clever solutions almost every time. The printer is dead; the mindset stuck.

### How I live

**Own the stack.** Where I can run it myself, I do: FreshRSS instead of a feed service, Jellyfin instead of a streaming subscription, my own DNS. Renting is convenient right up until the terms change.

**Privacy is the default.** GrapheneOS on the phone, self-hosted services at home, nothing handed over just because it's easier.

**Repair before replace.** The [Framework](/local-setup/) is repairable by design, and the server is older hardware doing real work. Buy for years, not for now.

**Write to think.** I don't write because I've got it figured out; I write to find out whether I do. Half of learning is explaining it, and [this site](/about/) is where that happens.
